Open Stance / menu prototype

Click, or use arrow keys, Enter and Esc. Esc is B / Back.

Example content. Fighter names, ratings, dates and bracket results are placeholders. Screens and flow follow GDD 7.5 to 7.8; items tagged Future (Challenges, Referee Mode, Online, Replays) are shown greyed. Sparring and Tournament use premade fighters; the created fighter (Jae-won Choi) is part of Career only. In the match, press Enter to score a random technique and Esc to pause.
